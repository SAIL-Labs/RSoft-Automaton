Should expect degeneracies in both amplitude and phase, but not at the same time; amplitude may be degenerate but phase mightn't be.

What the loss function then checks is the total power conservation; for the mode selective mode we want to maximise the power through the mode selective core and minimise the sum of powers for other non-mode selective cores/modes, meanwhile maximising the total output power over the entire lantern (this means conserving the portion of light partitioned to higher order modes).
